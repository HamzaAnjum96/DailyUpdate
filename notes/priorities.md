# Editorial priorities

Working notes for putting the weekly brief together: what to check first, what makes the cut, and what leads each section.

These priorities shape the choices; they never appear on the deck. Card copy doesn't say who an item matters to or why it was picked: no "for Cheetham Hill", "your route", "local to you". Name stops, roads and venues only where the item's own facts need them. This file isn't published: the Pages workflow deploys only `index.html`, `templates.html`, `assets/` and `data/`.

## How to apply them

1. Run the **weekly checks** below before the general research.
2. Anything on these routes and roads, or at these venues, inside the brief's range is in by default, even if it's smaller than a citywide brief would usually carry. Drop it only if nothing changes for people travelling or attending.
3. These items lead their section. The priority card (hazard band, cover teaser) goes to whichever item hits these routes hardest, unless something citywide is clearly bigger.
4. Ratings stay honest. IMPACT measures the disruption itself, not how much it matters here.
5. When a citywide disruption leaves these routes running, say so in that card's copy as a plain network fact ("Bury line trams run as normal"). Don't make an "all clear" card.

## Trams: Bury line from Queens Road

Queens Road (zone 2) is the last stop before Victoria.

- **Into town:** Queens Road → Victoria → Shudehill → Market Street → Piccadilly Gardens → Piccadilly (Bury–Ashton trams). Altrincham–Bury trams run Victoria → Shudehill → Market Street → St Peter's Square → Deansgate-Castlefield. Trafford Centre–Crumpsall trams use the Second City Crossing: Victoria → Exchange Square → St Peter's Square.
- **To Bury:** Queens Road → Abraham Moss → Crumpsall → Bowker Vale → Heaton Park → Prestwich → Besses o' th' Barn → Whitefield → Radcliffe → Bury.
- **Services calling (timetable from 14 Sep 2026):** Bury–Ashton-under-Lyne all day, which also runs direct to Etihad Campus; Altrincham–Bury part-day (weekdays 07:00–20:00, Saturdays 09:00–18:30); Trafford Centre–Crumpsall all day.
- **Watch for:** works between Victoria and the city-centre stops, anything at Victoria, Bury-line closures (often Sundays and early mornings), and lifts out at Bury-line stops.
- When the Bury line is suspended, TfGM usually lets tram tickets be used on buses, including the 135. Name the buses from TfGM's own alert.
- Don't take service patterns from Wikipedia; its Queens Road page is out of date.

## Buses

**135** Piccadilly Gardens – Cheetham Hill – Whitefield – Bury. It runs along Corporation Street, then Cheetham Hill Road (Strangeways, Cheetham, the stop by Queens Road), then Bury Old Road (Cheetham Hill Shopping Centre, Heaton Park, Besses o' th' Barn), then Bury New Road (Whitefield) and Manchester Road into Bury. Buses run about every 10 minutes in the daytime and every 20 in the evening. Hourly night buses run on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, via Shudehill after midnight.

From the Queens Road junction into town, only the 41 and 135 carry on to Victoria, Shudehill and Piccadilly.

## Roads

- **Queens Road → Alan Turing Way (A6010).** This is the intermediate ring road. Queens Road runs east from Cheetham Hill Road through Collyhurst and Miles Platting, then Hulme Hall Lane and Alan Turing Way past the Etihad Stadium and Co-op Live. West of Cheetham Hill Road it carries on via Elizabeth Street and Great Cheetham Street East to Leicester Road in Broughton. Etihad and Co-op Live event nights load the whole corridor.
- **Cheetham Hill Road → Bury Old Road (A665).** The main road into town; it becomes Bury Old Road at Crescent Road. It's the 135's spine.
- **Bury New Road (A56).** Strangeways → Broughton → Prestwich → M60 J17 → Whitefield.
- **Middleton Road (A576).** From Bury Old Road through Crumpsall and Bowker Vale, alongside Heaton Park, to M60 J19. It closes beside the park on big event nights.
- **Rochdale Road (A664).** Collyhurst → Harpurhey → Blackley → M60 J20.
- **Mancunian Way (A57(M)).** The elevated road along the south of the city centre (A6 to the A56 Chester Road), and the way to the universities, hospitals and south Manchester. Manchester City Council runs it; closures are usually overnight.
- **M60 junctions 17–20.** J17 Prestwich/Whitefield (A56); J18 Simister Island, where the M60, M62 and M66 meet (works run to 2029–30); J19 Heaton Park/Middleton (A576 Middleton Road); J20 Blackley (A664 Rochdale Road).

## Sales and markets

Only sales and markets that aren't normally on: a set start and end, and big enough that people plan around them. If nothing qualifies, the brief runs without a sale card.

- **In:** Black Friday and Boxing Day sales, the Christmas markets, seasonal or one-off markets (festive, night, makers' and festival markets), and dated closing-down or opening sales at big stores.
- **Out:** always-on or rolling sales and clearance pages (an H&M or Zara sale, Currys clearance), voucher-code round-ups, undated "up to X% off" offers, and regular weekly or monthly markets unless this edition is different (a festive special, a new site).
- Take sale dates and discounts only from the retailer's or organiser's own announcement. Deal sites and press round-ups are leads, not sources. Count early-access days only when the retailer publishes them.
- A seasonal market that opens after the brief's dates goes in as a look-ahead the week before it opens, then as a dated card in its opening week.

Check:
- https://tfgm.com/plan-a-journey/events (Christmas markets dates, other big events)
- https://tfgm.com/manchester-christmas-markets (dates and travel; points to the council's page for sites and hours, which blocks fetching)
